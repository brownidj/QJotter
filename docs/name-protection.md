# Protecting QJotter

Prepared 9 November 2026. This is a practical plan, not completed registration.

1. Obtain a broader trade mark clearance search before substantial public launch
   or marketing spend. The preliminary Australian search found no exact QJotter
   match, but similar marks and earlier unregistered use still need consideration.
   In particular, investigate QJot and Jotter in the software field.
2. Secure qjotter.com and qjotter.app if still available; consider qjotter.com.au
   or qjotter.au if eligible. Register in your own or your company's account,
   enable two-factor authentication and auto-renewal, and retain recovery codes.
   Domain ownership does not substitute for a trade mark.
3. Prioritise a plain-word trade mark for QJOTTER, rather than only a logo.
   A word mark protects the name independently of its visual styling. Apply in
   the name of the person or legal entity intended to own the brand.
4. Likely relevant classes are Class 9 for downloadable note-taking apps and,
   if offering hosted software services, Class 42. Choose precise goods/services
   reflecting the actual offering; using a server alone does not automatically
   mean a Class 42 service is being sold. An attorney can settle the wording.
5. IP Australia's TM Headstart offers early examiner feedback. The current
   minimum government fees are AUD 330 per class across the assessment and
   conversion steps (AUD 660 for two classes), excluding amendments and any
   professional fees. It is not an infringement clearance service.
6. Use QJotter consistently, keep dated evidence of public use, and reserve
   matching store listings and social handles where useful. You may use TM
   before registration; use the registered symbol only after registration and
   within the scope of the registration.
7. Plan country-specific clearance and protection before overseas expansion.
   Australian registration does not automatically protect the name overseas.

No domains have been purchased and no trade mark application has been filed.
